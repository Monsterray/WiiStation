Achievement unlock toasts, round 3

This round upgrades your picks: Save Block (6), Disc Read (7) and Broadcast (8). It adds a heavily PS1-styled unlock and four set-complete animations, one in each style. Round 1 and round 2 are in this page's version history.

All of them play over a 640 × 480 game picture (the size WiiStation draws on the TV), with the title-safe area dashed. They slide, wipe and step. Nothing bounces or squashes. The names are examples.

Save Block II

It plays like a PS1 save:

  • The icon is a 16 × 16 save icon with three frames (front, turned, side). It turns the way PS1 memory-card icons do.
  • The blue is made of bands with a 2 × 2 dither on top, the way 15-bit PS1 colour shows a gradient.
  • The count reads like a card block ("BLOCK 12/40"). The new block flashes twice and the total points step up.

Disc Read II

The disc slides in at rest, then spins up the way a PS1 drive does: slow at first, faster and faster, with "SPIN UP" blinking beside it. At full speed the motion blurs, the panel opens and the red laser reads the text in. To leave, it keeps the same speed and slides off the screen.

Broadcast II

It now has two tiers, like a sports graphic. The steps are:

  • The gold tab arrives first, with the same save icon as 6. Then the strip wipes out from behind it.
  • A thin top tier wipes up with the game's name.
  • The points box counts up, and a glint runs along the gold line.
  • The earned count goes up and the new block flashes.

It leaves in the reverse order.

Polygon

A PS1 game's "item get" screen:

  • The box opens from a line in four chunky steps and flashes white, like a BIOS screen.
  • A low-poly trophy is drawn one flat-shaded triangle at a time, like geometry popping in. It then turns in coarse steps, and its vertices jitter by a pixel (no sub-pixel precision).
  • The banded gold letters drop in one at a time, with hard black shadows, and a ▶ prompt blinks.
  • It leaves like an old TV turning off: it collapses to a line, then to nothing.

Top centre
Timing
0.3 s open, 0.7 s build, 3.5 s hold, 0.5 s TV-off
On the Wii
16 flat triangles through GX with a rotation matrix rounded to whole steps; the letters are the menu font with a gradient texture. It is the biggest of the four, so use it for rare unlocks only, or as a "PS1" theme choice.
